## Getting here

Alkapuri Hall is a short ride from Vadodara railway station. The lot behind the hall posts a festival rate after 4 p.m. Bike parking is along the side lane. Rideshares should use the RC Dutt Road door, not the lane.

## In the rooms

Both screens are step-free from the sidewalk. The Main Hall has 420 seats and a hearing loop. The Studio has 90 seats and no late entry once the first image is on screen. Caption devices are at the box office; ask when you collect your ticket.

## Lobby

The bar opens with the first doors of the day and stays open twenty minutes after the last credit. Opening night adds a reception after *Salt on the Lens* for festival-pass holders. Outside food stays outside. The lobby is quiet enough to talk and loud enough that you will not whisper.

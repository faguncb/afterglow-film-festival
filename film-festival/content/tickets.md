Seats are held under the name on your request. Collect them at the box office on Ash Street from noon on the day of your first screening. The lobby is cashless; the box office takes cards.

Tell us in the note if you need a companion seat, captions, or a seat near the aisle. The Lantern has step-free entry to both screens.
